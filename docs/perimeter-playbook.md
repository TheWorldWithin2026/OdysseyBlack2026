# Odyssey Black — Perimeter Playbook (30 days)

**Goal:** turn Perimeter Center (Dunwoody / Sandy Springs / Brookhaven) into a steady source of corporate accounts.
**Honest framing:** no plan can *guarantee* clients. This one maximizes the number of qualified conversations you have, and shows you which channel is working by day 14. Targets below are activity targets you control, not promised results.

## Why Perimeter
- Central Perimeter led metro Atlanta office absorption in Q2 2026 (CBRE), so new companies keep arriving.
- Newly arrived companies have not yet chosen ground-transport vendors for visiting executives and guests.
- Dunwoody alone reports ~10 hotels / ~2,245 rooms; many run MARTA shuttles, which leaves a gap for private executive transport.
- Public-news snapshot (verify before use): Asbury Automotive (HQ to Sandy Springs), AIG, Newell Brands, Inspire Brands, TriNet, StubHub, Swisslog, Beon, Protomiq.

## The four channels, in priority order
1. **New-arrival companies (Tier A/B on the Radar).** Reach the *gatekeeper first*: executive assistant, office manager, facilities, events/HR relocation lead. Use Gatekeeper Finder (X-ray searches), log people only from public sources you can cite.
2. **Hotel concierge / front-desk referrals.** Six Perimeter hotels on the Radar. Visit in person with a one-page rate/service sheet; ask who owns ground-transport referrals.
3. **Existing-client referrals.** Ask every current client's EA for one introduction per week.
4. **Local networks.** Discover Dunwoody events and the MPI Georgia chapter for meeting planners.

## Weekly targets (activity, not outcomes)
| Week | Focus | Targets |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundation | Verify 4 Tier A moves from a 2nd source; 10 gatekeepers logged; 3 hotels visited |
| 2 | First contact | 10 gatekeeper-first messages; other 3 hotels; 2 Radar scans; 3-day follow-ups |
| 3 | Expand | Tier B: 6 gatekeepers + 10 messages; ask for introductions; 2 trial rides offered |
| 4 | Convert | Account proposal to every engaged company; one re-contact of non-responders; review which channel replied |

## Daily rhythm (45 min)
1. Open **Perimeter Radar**, run **Scan Now** (twice a week is enough). Research every NEW headline: who is the company, is the move real?
2. Pick 1 company; **Find Gatekeepers →** and log what you can verify.
3. Send 5 messages (gatekeeper-first, verified claims only through AI Outreach).
4. Update statuses; follow up anything 3 days old.

## Rules that protect your reputation
- Never state a fact in outreach you haven't verified (the app only uses operator-verified claims).
- Do not guess emails. Use only addresses you can source.
- A headline is a lead, not proof. Confirm the company and the move first.

## What the Radar can and cannot do
- **Can:** surface public headlines about Perimeter moves/leases and flag unseen ones as NEW.
- **Cannot:** see private leases, guarantee coverage, or supply contact details.
- For stronger coverage add: CBRE/JLL/Cushman Atlanta market-report subscriptions, Atlanta Business Chronicle alerts, Georgia Secretary of State new foreign-registration filings, and Google Alerts on "Perimeter Center" + "headquarters".

## Review at day 30
Count replies per channel. Double down on the channel with the best reply rate; drop the weakest. Export a backup (Backup tab) before restarting the sprint.
